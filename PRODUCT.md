# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Platform Admin**: Operates the platform, verifies cooperatives, activates modules, manages users.
- **Government Supervisor**: External oversight; reads aggregated dashboards and masked audit trails.
- **Cooperative Manager**: Runs one cooperative; approves loans, stock corrections, views dashboards.
- **Operational Staff**: Warehouse/field workers; fast mobile input, batch registration, weight checks, freshness scans, barcode movement.
- **Financing Partner**: External lender; filtered portfolio reports without raw operational data.
- **Cooperative Member**: End beneficiary; applies for financing, views own profile.

## Product Purpose

A multi-tenant cooperative digitalization platform that connects member identity, financing governance, and commodity operations across a network of cooperatives without breaking tenant isolation. Scresh, the flagship operational module, turns vegetable stock into traceable, scannable, finance-ready inventory.

## Brand Personality

Modern, friendly, operational, trustworthy, agricultural, enterprise-ready, mobile-first.

Voice: clear, field-facing Indonesian for operational screens; confident and governance-aware for manager/supervisor flows.

## Anti-references

- Generic enterprise admin dashboards with dense tables and no mobile consideration.
- Overly playful or decorative farm/agricultural clichés.
- Monochromatic gray SaaS interfaces that hide operational urgency.

## Design Principles

1. **Field-first, governance-backed**: Operational screens are fast and mobile; oversight screens are precise and filtered.
2. **Traceability by default**: Every batch, movement, and correction has identity, timestamp, and audit context.
3. **Tenant isolation is invisible but absolute**: Users never see data from other cooperatives.
4. **Action over analytics for staff**: Staff dashboards prioritize the next physical task, not charts.
5. **Honest AI support**: Freshness scanning is decision support with mandatory human confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Mobile touch targets ≥44px; preferred 48px.
- Reduced-motion alternative for all animations.
- Color is never the sole carrier of status; labels always accompany badges.
